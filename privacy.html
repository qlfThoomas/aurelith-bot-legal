Privacy Policy

Aurelith Studios Bot · Effective date: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Discord bot operated by Aurelith Studios (the "Bot") collects, why, and what you can do about it. We collect as little as possible and never sell your data.

1. Information we collect

  • Discord identifiers: your Discord user ID, username and display name, and the IDs of the servers, channels, roles and messages involved when you use a command or when a moderation action concerns you.
  • Command input: the options you type into the Bot's commands, only to answer them.
  • Moderation records: warnings, mutes, kicks and bans issued in our community server, with the reason, the staff member and the date.
  • Linked Roblox account (optional): if you choose to link your Roblox account, your Roblox user ID and username, and the game information you ask the Bot to display (for example your progress in our games).

The Bot does not read or store the content of your messages beyond what is needed to run a command or a moderation action.

2. Why we use it

  • to run the Bot's features and answer your commands;
  • to keep our community safe (moderation and abuse prevention);
  • to give you roles or rewards linked to our Roblox experiences, if you linked your account.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. It is only processed by the services the Bot needs to work: Discord, Roblox (for linked accounts) and our hosting provider. It may be shared if the law requires it.

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider and may be processed outside your country. We keep linked-account data until you unlink your account or ask us to delete it, and moderation records for up to 12 months, unless a longer period is needed to deal with serious or repeated abuse.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or object to its processing. You can unlink your Roblox account at any time. To make a request, contact us (see below); we answer within 30 days. In France, you can also file a complaint with the CNIL (www.cnil.fr).

6. Children

The Bot is meant for people who meet Discord's minimum age in their country. We do not knowingly collect more data from younger users; if you believe a child's data was collected, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect the data we keep, but no system is completely secure.

8. Changes

We may update this Policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: contact us on the Aurelith Studios Discord server or by email at contact.thoomas@gmail.com.
